# Code of Conduct

LLMtoJev is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Contributors should:

- Treat other people and their work with respect.
- Give constructive feedback and accept it gracefully.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and disruptive behavior.
- Keep technical disagreements focused on evidence and project outcomes.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior threatens the safety or health of the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's contact information on GitHub. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential where possible.
